Read aloud

Master Board reads the text with the voices of Windows.

  • From the start: reads the page and goes on with the next ones.
  • Selected text: drag a rectangle over the text to read; it is marked in yellow.
  • From the click: reads from the word you touch.
  • Play, Pause, Stop and Next page.
Also in Edit → Read and dictate → Read aloud (Ctrl + Shift + L).
