AI

Use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and other AI without leaving the board.

  • Actions: Summary, Translation, Create image, Remove background, Generative erase and Transcribe.
  • You choose a part of the page or the whole PDF; Master Board attaches it, writes the instruction and sends it.
  • The page of the AI opens at the side, with your session kept; the list at the top changes the AI.
  • The answer comes back by dragging it to the page, with right click → Put on the page, or by downloading it.
  • When the free uses run out, a message offers to go on with another AI. The AI are edited in Preferences → AI.
Some actions need you to sign in to the AI, for example to create images.
