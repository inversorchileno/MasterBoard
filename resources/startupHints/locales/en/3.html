Pen

To write and draw freehand, with the mouse or a digital pen.

  • Five tips: ballpoint pen, fountain pen, calligraphy pen, graphite pencil and marker.
  • The width with the slider or by typing the number.
  • Colors: 15 in one click, the wheel for any color and the eyedropper to take a color from the screen.
  • The opacity makes the stroke more or less transparent.
  • Recognize shapes: a circle, rectangle or line drawn by hand becomes perfect.
  • Recognize and convert handwriting (Windows): a moment after you stop writing, the words written become a text box of the same size and color.
The chosen color is shown on the pen icon of the palette.
