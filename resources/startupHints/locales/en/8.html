Selector: arrow / lasso, and hand

To choose, move and change what is on the page.

  • Arrow: drag a rectangle to select; over a PDF it copies its text.
  • Lasso: draw a free line around what you want to select.
  • What is selected is moved, enlarged, turned, copied or deleted with the buttons of its frame.
  • The hand moves the page; the zoom is at the bottom right (or Ctrl + mouse wheel).
  • When strokes, shapes or arrows are selected, a panel below changes their color, size and opacity (Undo reverts it).
Search (top right) finds words in the PDF.
