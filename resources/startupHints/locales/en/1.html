Welcome to Master Board

A board and a PDF reader to write, draw, present and study. This guide shows each tool: choose a topic in the index at the right.

  • At the top are the menus: Home (open, save, print), Mode (board, double board, desktop), Edit, Tools, Customize and About.
  • Below them, the options bar: it shows the options of the tool you chose.
  • At the right, the tool palette; at the left, the pages of the document.
  • At the top right: Lock canvas, Index of the PDF, Search and the switch to the Desktop mode.
You can open this guide again whenever you want in About → Tips and tricks (F1).
