Measurements

Distances, perimeters and areas, on the board and on the desktop.

  • The first time, the window of the measure asks for the unit (mm, cm, m, km, inches, feet, yards or miles) and a reference line that measures 1 of that unit.
  • Distance: a click at the start and one at the end (the reference line too).
  • Perimeter and Area: a click at each corner and a double click at the last one, Enter or Finish measure; Escape starts again. Then Modify lets the corners be moved.
  • The window is moved by dragging it; Restart asks for the unit and the reference again, and the X ends the measurements.
With another tool, the window keeps the last measure. On the board the zoom does not change the measures. Edit → Canvas measurements shows rulers in pixels at the top and at the left. If the tool palette covers a ruler, the ruler moves to the other side.
