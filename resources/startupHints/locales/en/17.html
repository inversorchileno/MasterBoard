Pages, templates and PDF

Everything at the right of the options bar.

  • Add page, Delete page, Previous, Next and Rotate.
  • Template style: color of the sheet, grid or lines, and 21 templates (calendars, lists, Cornell notes, staff…). The scroll direction (also in Customize) puts the sheets one under the other, one beside the other, or two by two as an open notebook, downward or sideways.
  • Clear page erases what was drawn; Lock canvas stops changes made by mistake.
  • Home: Save (the annotations stay in the PDF), Save as, Print, Extract pages and Recognize text (OCR) of a scanned PDF.
  • Each document opens in a tab; tabs are reordered by dragging them. Mode → Double board shows two at once.
  • In the list of pages (left), the current page shows close (delete) and clone at its top, and a star at its bottom to mark it as a favorite; above, All / Favorites shows only the favorites.
  • A line that goes onto the sheet beside is cut: each part is kept on its own page.
The Index (top right) shows the titles of the PDF: a click goes to their page.
