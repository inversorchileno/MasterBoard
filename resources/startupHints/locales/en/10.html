Stamp

To correct and mark in one click.

  • The Stamps button opens all the stamps (Approved, As is, Confidential, Draft…): choose one and click on the page.
  • Your PNG: choose a picture of yours and use it as a stamp, for example your signature or logo.
  • Remove: a click on a stamp deletes it.
Your PNG is empty each time the program opens: choose the picture again.
