Customize and shortcuts

LibertyBoard your way.

  • Tools: all the tools, also those you took out of the palette.
  • Customize: tick the buttons you want to see, on the desktop and on the board; the menu stays open while ticking.
  • Move items: put the buttons in order by dragging them; Apply shows the order without closing the window.
  • Bar style: the color inside and the color of the edge of the tool palettes, their opacity and their corners, from square to round.
  • About → Shortcuts: all the keyboard shortcuts; choose a function, press the new keys and Assign. When they clash with Windows or another function, a message explains it.
Preferences (in Home) has the language, the theme (dark, white, grey or as Windows), the pen pressure, the desktop border and the AI.
